About Fumes

Fumes is a private, non-commercial development project by Pasi Saarinen. It shows a single gauge that tells you whether your spending is on pace for the current pay period.

Fumes is currently in development and used only by its developer. It is provided as-is, without any warranty, and may change or stop working at any time.

Not financial advice

Fumes gives a general indication of spending pace. It is not financial advice, and it does not replace your bank's own services or statements.
