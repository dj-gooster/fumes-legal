Privacy Policy

Fumes · Last updated 8 November 2026

Fumes is a private project in development. It is not available to the public. Only the developer's own bank accounts are connected.

Who is responsible

Data controller: Pasi Saarinen, Finland.
Contact: privacy@fumesapp.com

What data

  • Account details (account name and number)
  • Account balances
  • Transactions (date, amount, and payee or payer)

Access is read-only. Fumes cannot make payments or change anything in your bank.

Why

To explore and test what can be done with account and transaction data through open banking.

Legal basis

Your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time through your bank or by contacting us.

Recipients

The data is received through Enable Banking Oy, a licensed account information service provider. It is not sold, or shared for advertising.

How long it is kept

Only as long as needed for the purpose above, and deleted on request. Bank consent ends automatically after at most 180 days.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, and to lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).
