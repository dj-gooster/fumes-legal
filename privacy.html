Privacy Policy

Fumes · Last updated 31 October 2026

Fumes is a private development project. It is not a public service, and it is currently used only by its developer, with his own bank accounts.

Who is responsible

Data controller: Pasi Saarinen, Finland.
Contact: macrame.upping.0e@icloud.com

What data Fumes uses

With your consent, Fumes reads the following through Enable Banking Oy, a licensed account information service provider:

  • Account details (account name and identifier)
  • Account balances
  • Transactions (date, amount, and payee or payer)

Fumes has read-only access. It cannot make payments or change anything in your bank.

Why

The data is used only to show your spending pace for the current pay period: whether you are using your discretionary money faster or slower than expected.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on the developer's own devices and private systems. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

How long it is kept

Data is kept only as long as needed for the purpose above. You can ask for it to be deleted at any time.

Your consent and rights

Access is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through your bank or by contacting us. Bank consent also expires automatically, after at most 180 days. Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, and to lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Changes

This policy will be updated if Fumes becomes available to other users.
